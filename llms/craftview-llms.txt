# Craftview

> Craftview Software GmbH is a Frankfurt-headquartered European software group building ERP and project-management software for the skilled trades. It operates a portfolio of long-established regional ERP brands across Germany, Belgium, the Netherlands and France, serving 25,000+ active customers with 330+ employees across 11 locations and 41 key European regions.
